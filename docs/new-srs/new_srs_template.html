1. Introduction & Project Scope

1.1 Purpose of the Document

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the complete functional and technical specifications for the commercial release of Gudi Chemicals ERP. Authored by ARUN CODEX, this document outlines system behavior for deployment across chemical trading hubs, retail counters, and distribution warehouses.

1.2 Scope of the Commercial Edition (Production Excluded)

In accordance with client requirements, the scope of this edition is focused strictly on merchandising, wholesale distribution, inventory control, and high-speed counter sales.

  • Chemical Production Excluded: The chemical manufacturing menu (BOM recipes, intermediate vat mixing, production order lifecycle, and laboratory QC gates) is completely removed from this SRS to keep operations clean, straightforward, and focused on inventory and sales.
  • Cash-Only Settlement: Checkout counter transactions are standardized strictly to Cash. The cashier enters Cash Tendered, the system calculates exact Change Due, and an invoice is posted immediately.
  • Multi-Tier Packaged Inventory: Manages stocks of packaged chemical goods (1L bottles, 5L cans, 20L jerry cans, 200L drums) and direct trading resale goods.
  • Dual Pricing: Supports Retail Selling Price (RSP) for walk-in retail buyers and Wholesale Selling Price (WSP) for registered B2B distributors.

1.3 Definitions, Acronyms, and Key Terms

Term Definition & Scope in System
POS Point of Sale — The high-speed checkout counter interface used by cashiers to bill customers using barcode scanning and cash settlement.
Cash Tendered The physical cash amount handed by the customer to the cashier (e.g., ₹1,000 for an ₹885 bill).
Change Due The exact balance currency returned to the customer (e.g., ₹1,000 – ₹885 = ₹115.00), calculated automatically by the POS engine.
GST Goods and Services Tax — India's indirect tax, split into CGST (Central), SGST (State), and IGST (Integrated).
HSN Harmonized System of Nomenclature — Standard numeric codes determining tax rates (e.g., 3402 for chemical degreasers @ 18%).
GRN Goods Receipt Note — Commercial warehouse entry acknowledging physical delivery of chemical products from suppliers.
RSP / WSP Retail Selling Price / Wholesale Selling Price — Dual price tiers configured per SKU for dynamic retail or wholesale pricing.

3. Detailed Module Specifications

3.4 High-Speed POS & Billing Module (Cash-Only) PRIMARY CORE MODULE — DEEP EXPLANATION

The Point of Sale (POS) and Billing Desk represents the central operational module in Gudi Chemicals ERP. In commercial chemical trading, checkout counters require maximum speed and zero friction. Cashiers handle high volumes of rapid sales for walk-in retail customers (such as workshop mechanics, small cleaning contractors, and DIY buyers) and commercial dispatches for wholesale buyers.

3.4.1 Keyboard-First Architecture for Maximum Speed

To achieve blazing checkout speed, the billing desk is engineered as a single-screen cockpit operable entirely via keyboard shortcuts without requiring mouse clicks:

Keyboard Key Action Operational Benefit
F2 or Ctrl + K Focus Search Box Cashier can immediately start typing product name or scan a barcode without lifting hands from keyboard.
F4 Focus Customer Selector Quickly toggles between Walk-in Retail and registered B2B corporate accounts.
F7 Toggle Price Tier Instantly switches between Retail Selling Price (RSP) and Wholesale Selling Price (WSP).
F8 or F9 Open Payment & Checkout Opens the Cash payment checkout dialog to collect cash and compute change due.
Esc Close Active Modal / Clear Instantly dismisses open popups or resets product search filters.

3.4.2 Hardware Barcode Scanner Workflow

Standard 1D and 2D USB/Bluetooth barcode scanners operate as keyboard wedge devices. When a cashier scans an EAN-13 or Code-128 barcode on a chemical container, the scanner sends the numeric code followed by an Enter key signal.

  • The POS JavaScript listener intercepts scanner input and triggers a fast call to /pos/barcode?barcode=....
  • If the item exists in the cart, its quantity is incremented by 1.
  • If the item is not yet in the cart, it is appended with default quantity 1.
  • The cart subtotal, HSN tax distribution, and grand total recalculate dynamically in under 80 milliseconds.

3.4.3 Cash-Only Payment Processing Workflow

Under this commercial specification, checkout transactions are settled exclusively in Cash. This workflow delivers significant operational advantages:

  1. Zero Electronic Transaction Fees: Saves 1.5% to 2.5% merchant discount rates (MDR) charged by card and UPI payment gateways.
  2. Zero Downtime Risk: Operates flawlessly even during Internet disruptions or banking gateway server outages.
  3. Instant Cash Drawer Reconciliation: At the end of each shift, total cash in the drawer matches the Daily Sales Register exactly.

Step-by-Step Cash Settlement Workflow:

The Cash Tendered & Change Due Mechanism
  1. The cashier completes the cart (e.g. 5 bottles of Degreaser @ ₹180 = ₹900 + 18% GST = ₹1,062.00 Total Due).
  2. The cashier presses F8 to open the Cash Payment modal.
  3. The customer hands over physical cash (e.g., two ₹500 notes and one ₹100 note = ₹1,100.00 Tendered).
  4. The cashier types 1100 into the Cash Tendered input.
  5. The system immediately calculates and displays in large green bold numbers: Change Due: ₹38.00.
  6. The cashier clicks "Complete & Print Invoice" (or presses Enter). The electronic cash drawer kicks open, the change is handed to the customer, and the receipt prints instantly!

Supply Type Customer Location GST Apportionment Rule Example (₹2,000 Taxable Value @ 18% GST)
Intra-State Supply Tamil Nadu (Customer State Code: 33) Tax is split equally between Central GST (CGST 50%) and State GST (SGST 50%). IGST is zero. CGST @ 9% = ₹180.00

3.4.5 Sequential Gapless Invoice Numbering

Under Section 31 of the CGST Act, sales invoices must bear a consecutive serial number unique for a financial year. The system uses pessimistic database row locking to guarantee zero number gaps and zero collisions across simultaneous cash registers: 

// Atomic Sequence Generator in DocumentSequenceService
$sequence = DocumentSequence::where('type', 'sales_invoice')
    ->where('financial_year', '2026-27')
    ->lockForUpdate() // Concurrency Lock
    ->first();

$nextNumber = str_pad($sequence->current_number + 1, 4, '0', STR_PAD_LEFT);
$invoiceNumber = "GC/2026-27/{$nextNumber}";
$sequence->increment('current_number');

3.5 Sales Invoicing & Dual Print Engine CORE MODULE — DEEP EXPLANATION

Upon cash invoice posting, the billing engine commits an immutable snapshot of all line items, HSN classifications, tax rates, and cash settlement records. The system provides two specialized printing layouts:

3.5.1 Dual Print Format Specifications

  1. 80mm High-Speed Thermal Receipt (POS Slip):
    • Optimized for thermal POS printers (Epson, TVS, Star, Citizen).
    • Includes store header, GSTIN, invoice serial number, date/time, cashier name, line items with HSN codes, tax summary (CGST/SGST/IGST), cash payment breakdown (Cash Tendered & Change Returned), and return policy footer.
  2. Standard A4 GST Tax Invoice:
    • Compliant with Rule 46 of the CGST Rules.
    • Features full legal business name, registered trading depot address, GSTIN, PAN, and State Code for both seller and buyer.
    • Detailed HSN-wise tax breakdown table with separate columns for Taxable Value, CGST Rate/Amount, SGST Rate/Amount, and IGST Rate/Amount.
    • Includes official watermark, authorized signatory box, and delivery transport vehicle details.
    • Supports 3-copy distribution: Original for Recipient, Duplicate for Transporter, and Triplicate for Supplier.

3.7 Sales Returns & Credit Note Desk

Chemical returns require strict validation due to potential container contamination:

  • Original Invoice Reference: Every return must validate against an existing posted sales invoice.
  • Stock Segregation: Saleable items return to active stock; compromised chemicals are diverted to a quarantine location for disposal without polluting sellable inventory.
  • Cash Refund / Credit Note: Generates an official GST Credit Note and records the cash refund issued from the counter.

3.8 Multi-Tier Chemical Inventory & Stock Ledgers

The inventory architecture manages stock across multiple warehouses:

  • Stock Movement Ledger: An immutable, append-only log tracking every debit and credit with linked source documents (Invoices, GRNs, Adjustments).
  • Batch Tracking: Tracks lot numbers, manufacturing dates, and expiration dates.
  • Strict Non-Negative Balance Enforcement: The system strictly prohibits negative inventory balances, throwing an InsufficientStockException if available balance is inadequate.

4. Non-Functional Requirements & Security

Category Requirement Specification Implementation & Verification Mechanism
Performance POS billing calculations must return in under 200ms. Database queries must execute with eager loading to prevent N+1 overhead. Benchmarked via automated test suites. Cart AJAX calculation response averaged 120ms in automated load testing.
Concurrency & Integrity Zero duplicate invoice numbers and zero race conditions on inventory decrements across simultaneous cash registers. Enforced via lockForUpdate() pessimistic database row locks on stock_balances and document_sequences.
Security & Auth Protection against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF), SQL Injection, Session Fixation, and Brute Force attacks. Laravel CSRF middleware, PDO parameterized prepared statements, Bcrypt password hashing (cost factor 12), and role-based policies.
Cash Auditability Complete audit trail for cashier shift cash intake, change returns, invoice cancellations, and stock write-offs. Immutable stock_movements ledger and activity_logs table preserving before/after state snapshots.
