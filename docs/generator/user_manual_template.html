2.1 Anatomy of the Billing Screen

The billing desk is divided into intuitive, high-visibility sections:

  1. Top Control Bar: Displays active Warehouse location selector, Customer selector dropdown, Quick Add Customer button (+), and Price Tier toggle (Retail / Wholesale).
  2. Product Search & Barcode Input: Search box with keyboard shortcut F2. Instantly searches across 4,000+ chemical items or accepts hardware barcode scanner scans.
  3. Product Catalog Grid: Visual grid showing fast-moving packaged chemicals with real-time stock badges and quick-add buttons.
  4. Active Order Cart: Displays added line items, unit prices, quantity modifiers (+/-), discount boxes, HSN codes, and line totals.
  5. Real-Time Tax & Total Box: Displays Subtotal, Discount, Taxable Value, CGST/SGST (Intra-state) or IGST (Inter-state), Round-off amount, and Grand Total.
  6. Action Buttons: Clear Cart (Esc) and Pay & Generate Invoice (F8).

2.3 Walk-in Retail Billing (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Select or Scan Products

- Point your USB barcode scanner at the product container barcode (e.g., Industrial Degreaser 1L bottle). The item immediately jumps into the cart with quantity 1.
- Alternatively, press F2 and type the first 3 letters of the product (e.g., deg), then click the product card or press Enter.

Step 2: Adjust Quantity

Use the + or - buttons in the cart row, or click directly into the quantity box to type a number (e.g., 5). The server immediately updates the line subtotal and tax amounts.

Step 3: Review Customer & Price Tier

For regular counter retail sales, leave the customer as "Walk-in Customer" and the tier as "Retail".

Step 4: Press F8 (Pay & Checkout)

The payment modal appears. If the customer hands you ₹1,000 cash for an ₹885 bill:
• Enter 1000 in the Cash Tendered box.
• The screen displays: Change Due: ₹115.00.

Step 5: Click "Complete & Print Invoice"

The transaction is committed atomically. Physical inventory is deducted immediately, and the thermal receipt or A4 tax invoice prints automatically!

2.4 B2B Wholesale Billing (Step-by-Step)

When billing corporate clients, chemical factories, or regional distributors, follow this professional workflow:

Step 1: Select the Registered B2B Customer

Press F4 and select the customer (e.g., Apex Chemical Industries Ltd). The system automatically displays their registered GSTIN, State Code, and available Credit Limit.

Step 2: Automatic Wholesale Pricing (WSP)

The system automatically switches the price tier to Wholesale (WSP). Notice that unit prices adjust to discounted bulk wholesale rates!

Step 3: Add Bulk Packaging Items

Select 20L Jerry Cans or 200L Drums. Add the requested drum quantities (e.g., 10 Drums).

Step 4: Multi-Mode Split Payment or Account Credit

In the payment modal (F8), you can split the settlement:
• Bank Transfer / NEFT: Enter ₹50,000 against company bank account.
• Customer Credit: Post the remaining balance (e.g., ₹25,000) to their ledger (Net 30 Days payment terms).

2.5 Understanding GST on Your Invoices (Simple English)

Indian GST operates under two simple location rules that Gudi Chemicals ERP calculates automatically:

2.6 Dual Print Formats: Thermal vs A4 Tax Invoice

Depending on customer requirements, choose the right format from the Invoices screen:

  • Thermal Slip (80mm): Click "Print Thermal". Ideal for cash sales, small vans, and retail customers. Quick, uses zero ink, and saves paper!
  • Standard A4 GST Tax Invoice: Click "Print A4". Essential for commercial transport vehicles, B2B tax credit input (ITC), and corporate accounts. Includes full seller/buyer GSTIN addresses, HSN tax summary, bank RTGS details, and authorized signature.

Chapter 3: Master Data Operations

3.1 Adding a New Chemical Product

To register a new raw chemical, packaging item, or finished bottled product:

  1. Go to Masters → Products in the left sidebar. Click "+ Add New Product".
  2. Item Details: Enter Item Name (e.g., Industrial Heavy-Duty Degreaser), SKU (e.g., FG-DEGREASE-5L), and Barcode.
  3. Item Classification: Select whether it is raw_material, semi_finished, finished_goods, or packaging.
  4. Pricing Tiers:
    • Cost Price (₹): Factory manufacturing cost or purchase price.
    • Retail Price (₹): Selling price for walk-in retail counter buyers.
    • Wholesale Price (₹): Discounted bulk price for B2B distributors.
    • Min Selling Price (₹): Safety price floor below which no cashier can sell.
  5. Tax & HSN: Enter the 4-digit or 8-digit HSN code (e.g., 3402) and GST rate (18%).
  6. Click "Save Product". The item is instantly available in the POS billing catalog!

Chapter 5: Chemical Manufacturing & BOM Guide

5.1 Creating a Bill of Materials (Master Formula)

A Bill of Materials (BOM) is your chemical recipe. Navigate to Production → Formulas → Create:

  1. Header: Give the recipe a name (e.g., Standard Alkaline Cleaner v1) and base output batch size (e.g., 1,000 Liters).
  2. Expected Yield & Process Loss: Enter standard yield (e.g., 98%) and allowed evaporation loss (2%).
  3. Add Ingredients: Specify the exact quantity of each raw ingredient per 1,000L (e.g., 850L DM Water, 100L Caustic Soda Flakes, 50L Surfactant).
  4. Click "Save Formula".

5.2 Running a Production Order & Quality Control (QC)

Step 1: Create Production Order

Select the master formula and enter your planned batch output (e.g., 2,500 Liters). The system automatically multiplies ingredient requirements by 2.5!

Step 2: Mixing & In-Process State

Mark order as "In Production". Operators consume raw ingredients from active warehouse lots.

Step 3: Laboratory Quality Control (QC)

Plant chemist enters laboratory test measurements: pH level, Specific Gravity, and Viscosity. If parameters pass, mark status as "QC Passed".

Step 4: Finalize Batch

Click "Finalize Production Order". In one single click, raw chemical stock is deducted, finished chemical inventory is credited, and cost per liter is recorded!

Chapter 6: Purchasing & Goods Receipt (GRN)

When raw chemicals arrive at the plant gate:

  1. Navigate to Purchases → Goods Receipt (GRN) → Create.
  2. Select the Vendor and reference Purchase Order.
  3. Enter received quantities, supplier lot number, manufacturing date, and expiry date.
  4. Click "Post Goods Receipt". Stock increments immediately in warehouse inventory!

Chapter 10: Troubleshooting & Quick FAQ

Issue Encountered Probable Cause How to Resolve
"Insufficient Stock" error when adding to cart Available warehouse quantity is lower than cart requested units. Check stock balance under Inventory. Perform a GRN or production finalization to increase sellable stock.
Barcode scanner does not add item Scanner USB cable loose or barcode not registered in Product Master. Test scanner in Notepad. If code types properly, ensure the barcode is linked to the product under Masters → Products.
Thermal slip prints blank or illegible Thermal roll inserted upside down or print head dirty. Flip paper roll so heat-sensitive coated side faces printer head. Clean head with isopropyl alcohol swab.
Wrong tax split on B2B invoice Customer State Code is set incorrectly. Edit customer profile under Masters → Customers. Ensure State is set to Maharashtra for CGST/SGST or other state for IGST.
