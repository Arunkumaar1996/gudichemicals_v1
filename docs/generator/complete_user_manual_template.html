Press Enter or click the button. Place the cash in the drawer, hand the ₹38 change to the customer, and hand over the printed invoice!

Chapter 8: Chemical Products Master & Dual Pricing

8.1 Product Attributes Guide

  • SKU (Stock Keeping Unit): Unique identifier (e.g. FG-DEGREASE-1L, FG-FLOOR-5L).
  • Barcode: 12 or 13-digit EAN/UPC code scanned by the hardware barcode scanner.
  • Item Classification:
    • finished_goods: Bottled cleaning products (Floor cleaner, toilet cleaner, etc.).
    • packaging: Empty HDPE bottles (1L), jerry cans (5L/20L), caps, and induction seals.
    • raw_material: Industrial chemical raw ingredients (Acids, LABSA, surfactants).
  • Dual Pricing Tiers:
    • Retail Selling Price (RSP): Standard counter price for walk-in retail buyers.
    • Wholesale Selling Price (WSP): Discounted price for B2B dealers and bulk distributors.
    • Min Selling Price: Absolute price floor below which no cashier can sell.
  • Taxation: HSN Code (e.g. 3402) and statutory GST rate (18.00%).

ARUN CODEX — Web Developer | Designer | Innovator Gudi Chemicals ERP — Chapter 11: Production Orders

Chapter 11: Production Orders & Laboratory QC Gates

Under Production → Orders, plant operators manage the complete chemical manufacturing lifecycle from batch initiation to laboratory testing and finished goods stock finalization.

__SCREENSHOT_10__
Figure 11.1: Production Orders Desk showing Batch Lifecycle (Draft → In Production → QC → Finalized)

11.1 The 4-Stage Production Lifecycle

  1. Draft & Planning: The order is scheduled. The system dynamically scales required raw ingredients based on planned output volume (e.g. 2.5x for 2,500L) and checks warehouse raw material availability.
  2. In Production: Raw chemical stocks are reserved, mixing vats are loaded, and compounding begins.
  3. Quality Control (QC Gate): Plant chemists test sample specimens against laboratory specifications:
    • pH Level (e.g. 1.5 – 2.5 for acid descalers, 9.5 – 11.0 for alkaline cleaners).
    • Specific Gravity (e.g. 1.08 ± 0.02 g/cm³).
    • Viscosity (Centipoise @ 25°C).
  4. Finalized: In a single atomic click, raw chemical stock is deducted, finished bottled goods are added to inventory, and unit production cost is calculated!

11.2 Step-by-Step Batch Finalization

Step 1: Record QC Measurements

Click "Record QC" on the active batch. Enter observed pH and specific gravity. Mark QC as Passed.

Step 2: Finalize Production Order

Click "Finalize Batch". The finished bottled cleaning products are immediately available on the POS billing desk for sale!

12.1 Purchasing Invariants

The Stock Increment Rule
Stock levels do not increase when a Purchase Order is created. Inventory increases strictly upon posting a Goods Receipt Note (GRN) after chemical containers physically arrive and are inspected at the depot gate.

Step 3: Post Goods Receipt

Click "Post GRN". The raw chemical or packaged product stock increments immediately in warehouse inventory!

Chapter 13: Sales Returns & GST Credit Notes

Under Sales → Returns, cashiers and customer service staff handle customer returns, container replacements, and credit notes.

__SCREENSHOT_12__
Figure 13.1: Sales Returns and Credit Note Management Desk with Original Invoice Linking

13.1 Key Controls & Stock Segregation

  • Mandatory Invoice Verification: Returns must validate against an original posted invoice number.
  • Saleable vs Damaged Classification:
    • Saleable: Sealed, undamaged bottles return to active sellable inventory.
    • Damaged / Contaminated: Diverted to a designated Quarantine area for destruction without polluting sellable stock.
  • GST Credit Note: Generates a legal credit note adjusting GST tax liability and issuing a cash refund or ledger credit.

13.2 Processing a Return

Step 1: Enter Original Invoice Number

Click "+ New Return". Type the invoice number (e.g. GC/2026-27/0001). The original items load automatically.

Step 2: Specify Quantity & Stock Condition

Enter returned units and mark condition as Saleable or Damaged.

Step 3: Issue Credit Note & Refund

Click "Process Return". The customer is refunded cash, and stock is updated accordingly.
