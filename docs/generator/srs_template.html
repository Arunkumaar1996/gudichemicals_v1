1. Introduction & Project Scope

1.1 Purpose of the Document

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) establishes the complete functional and technical specification for Gudi Chemicals ERP (Release 1.0). Authored and architected by ARUN CODEX, this document serves as the formal blueprint for deployment, testing, operations, and future multi-warehouse scaling.

1.2 Project Scope & Objectives

Chemical merchandising involves volatile liquids, fractional unit handling, hazardous materials, and statutory tax burdens. Off-the-shelf point-of-sale packages cannot manage chemical formulation loss, dynamic dilution, or bulk liquid bottling. Gudi Chemicals ERP delivers an integrated enterprise platform that:

  • Enables ultra-fast, keyboard-driven POS billing with barcode scanner support.
  • Maintains strict Indian GST compliance with automated CGST/SGST/IGST tax allocation and gapless sequential numbering.
  • Enforces non-negative stock controls backed by an append-only stock movement audit ledger.
  • Tracks multi-stage chemical compounding from raw acid vats to packaged 1L, 5L, 20L, and 200L finished goods.
  • Automates quality assurance gates with parametric testing (pH, specific gravity, viscosity).

1.3 Definitions, Acronyms, and Abbreviations

Term Definition & Scope in System
BOM Bill of Materials — The master chemical recipe specifying raw chemicals, packaging materials, expected yield, and process loss tolerance.
POS Point of Sale — Fast-billing counter software used by cashiers to bill retail and wholesale customers with minimal keystrokes.
GST Goods and Services Tax — India's comprehensive indirect tax divided into CGST (Central), SGST (State), and IGST (Integrated).
HSN Harmonized System of Nomenclature — Standard numeric codes classifying chemical products and determining tax rates.
GRN Goods Receipt Note — Official document recording physical delivery of raw chemicals, creating inventory batches and updating stock.
QC Quality Control — Laboratory inspection of chemical properties (pH, density, viscosity) before batch release.
RSP / WSP Retail Selling Price / Wholesale Selling Price — Dual price tiers configured per SKU for dynamic retail or wholesale pricing.

2. Overall System Description & Architecture

2.1 High-Level Architecture

The software utilizes a robust Model-View-Controller (MVC) architecture built on Laravel 12. Business logic is strictly encapsulated inside domain service classes (InvoicePostingService, GstCalculationService, InventoryService, ProductionService) to guarantee atomic database transactions and high unit-test coverage.

2.2 User Roles & Permissions (RBAC)

The system configures granular permissions across 8 distinct roles:

Role Primary Responsibilities Key Module Access
Super Admin Complete administrative oversight, system settings, user creation. All 13 Modules without restriction.
Cashier Counter checkout, barcode scanning, split payment collection. POS Billing Desk, Invoices (Own Shift), Quick Customer Add.
Storekeeper Physical warehouse custody, goods receipts, stock counts. GRN Posting, Inventory Ledger, Stock Adjustments (Propose).
Production Mgr BOM formulations, production order scheduling, QC testing. Formulas, Production Orders, Batch Finalization, QC Gates.
Sales Manager B2B dispatch pricing, sales returns, customer credit management. POS, Invoices, Sales Returns, Customers, Sales Reports.
Accountant Financial ledgers, expense tracking, tax compliance. Expenses, Invoices, GSTR-1 Reports, Vendor Ledgers.

2.3 Non-Negotiable System Invariants

Architectural Invariants Guaranteed by ARUN CODEX
  1. Stock Ledger Immutability: Inventory levels can never be updated directly via raw SQL overwrite. Every alteration requires a dedicated movement log in stock_movements referencing a valid source transaction (Invoice, GRN, Production Order, or Adjustment).
  2. Pessimistic Concurrency Locking: Inventory deductions and invoice sequence increments acquire a lockForUpdate() row-lock inside an atomic transaction, preventing race conditions during concurrent cash register operations.
  3. Strict Non-Negative Balance Enforcement: Any transaction attempting to reduce stock below 0.00 immediately halts and throws an InsufficientStockException.
  4. Authoritative Server-Side Math: All cart calculations, HSN tax breakdowns, and round-offs are computed on the server before database commitment, preventing client-side price tampering.

3. Detailed Module Specifications

3.4 High-Speed POS & Billing Module PRIMARY CORE MODULE — DEEP EXPLANATION

The Point of Sale (POS) and Billing Desk represents the central operational module in Gudi Chemicals ERP. In industrial chemical trade, billing counters must accommodate both rapid cash sales for walk-in retail customers (such as workshop mechanics, small cleaning contractors, and DIY buyers) and large-scale wholesale dispatches (such as chemical distributors and industrial factories buying 200L drums and bulk jerry cans).

3.4.1 Keyboard-Driven POS Desk Architecture

To achieve maximum cashier speed, the POS screen is designed as a single-screen cockpit operable entirely via keyboard shortcuts without requiring mouse clicks:

Shortcut Key Action Executed Operational Benefit
F2 or Ctrl + K Focus SKU / Barcode Search Box Cashier can instantly start typing product name or scan a barcode without lifting hands from keyboard.
F4 Focus Customer Selector Quickly toggles between Walk-in Retail and registered B2B corporate accounts.
F7 Toggle Price Tier (Retail vs Wholesale) Instantly recalculates cart from Retail Selling Price (RSP) to Wholesale Selling Price (WSP).
F8 Open Payment & Checkout Modal Brings up the split payment dialog to collect Cash, UPI, Card, or Credit.
Esc Close Active Modal / Clear Search Instantly dismisses open popups or resets product search filters.

3.4.2 Hardware Barcode Scanner Workflow

Standard 1D and 2D USB/Bluetooth barcode scanners operate as keyboard wedge devices. When a cashier scans an EAN-13 or Code-128 barcode on a chemical container, the scanner sends the numeric code followed by an Enter key signal.

  • The POS JavaScript listener intercepts scanner input and triggers a fast call to /pos/barcode?barcode=....
  • If the item exists in the cart, its quantity is incremented by 1.
  • If the item is not yet in the cart, it is appended with default quantity 1.
  • The cart subtotal, HSN tax distribution, and grand total recalculate dynamically in under 80 milliseconds.
  • An audible success chime alerts the cashier that the scan was registered.

3.4.3 Dual-Tier Pricing Logic (Retail vs Wholesale)

Every product in the item master contains two distinct selling price columns:

  • Retail Selling Price (RSP): Applied by default for consumer walk-in purchases, single bottles, and small volume retail buyers. Includes higher profit margins.
  • Wholesale Selling Price (WSP): Applied automatically when a registered B2B customer is selected, or when toggled via F7 for bulk pallet purchases.
  • Minimum Selling Price (Floor Guard): To prevent unauthorized undercutting, cashiers cannot enter a manual discount that pushes the effective unit price below the configured Minimum Selling Price without supervisor authorization.

3.4.4 Comprehensive Indian GST Calculation Engine

Gudi Chemicals operates from Maharashtra (State Code 27). The system's GstCalculationService performs authoritative tax evaluations based on statutory Indian tax rules:

3.4.5 Split-Payment Processing Engine

Industrial transactions frequently combine immediate cash deposits with electronic payments or account credits. Gudi Chemicals ERP supports multiple settlement modes within a single invoice:

  • Cash: Records physical cash received and computes change due back to the customer.
  • UPI QR: Displays an on-screen dynamic UPI payment QR code for instant phone-based settlement (PhonePe, Google Pay, BHIM).
  • Debit / Credit Card: Records electronic card swipe reference numbers (RRN).
  • Bank Transfer (NEFT / RTGS): Dedicated for large B2B dispatches against commercial bank accounts.
  • Customer Credit: Permitted only for registered wholesale customers with available credit headroom. Automatically creates a debit entry in customer_ledgers.

3.4.6 Sequential Gapless Invoice Numbering with Concurrency Locks

Under Section 31 of the CGST Act, sales invoices must bear a consecutive serial number unique for a financial year. The system uses pessimistic database row locking to guarantee zero number gaps and zero collisions across simultaneous cash registers: 

// Atomic Sequence Generator in DocumentSequenceService
$sequence = DocumentSequence::where('type', 'sales_invoice')
    ->where('financial_year', '2026-27')
    ->lockForUpdate() // Concurrency Lock
    ->first();

$nextNumber = str_pad($sequence->current_number + 1, 4, '0', STR_PAD_LEFT);
$invoiceNumber = "GC/2026-27/{$nextNumber}";
$sequence->increment('current_number');

3.5 Sales Invoicing, Tax Invoices & Dual Print Engine CORE MODULE — DEEP EXPLANATION

Upon invoice posting, the billing engine commits an immutable snapshot of all line items, HSN classifications, tax rates, and split payment records. The system provides two specialized printing layouts:

3.5.1 Dual Print Format Specifications

  1. 80mm High-Speed Thermal Receipt (POS Slip):
    • Optimized for thermal POS printers (Epson, TVS, Star, Citizen).
    • Includes store header, GSTIN, invoice serial number, date/time, cashier name, line items with HSN codes, tax summary (CGST/SGST/IGST), split payments, and return policy footer.
  2. Standard A4 GST Tax Invoice:
    • Compliant with Rule 46 of the CGST Rules.
    • Features full legal business name, registered factory address, GSTIN, PAN, and State Code for both seller and buyer.
    • Detailed HSN-wise tax breakdown table with separate columns for Taxable Value, CGST Rate/Amount, SGST Rate/Amount, and IGST Rate/Amount.
    • Includes bank details for RTGS payments, official watermark, authorized signatory box, and transport vehicle details.
    • Supports 3-copy distribution: Original for Recipient, Duplicate for Transporter, and Triplicate for Supplier.

3.6 Sales Returns & Credit Note Engine

Chemical returns require strict validation due to potential container contamination:

  • Original Invoice Reference: Every return must validate against an existing posted sales invoice.
  • Stock Segregation: Saleable items return to active stock; compromised chemicals are diverted to a quarantine location for disposal or reprocessing.
  • Credit Note Issuance: Generates an official GST Credit Note adjusting the customer's ledger.

3.7 Multi-Tier Chemical Inventory & Batch Tracking

The inventory architecture classifies items into 5 distinct categories:

  1. raw_material: Industrial acids, alkalis, solvents, surfactants, and colorants.
  2. semi_finished: Bulk compounded liquid solutions awaiting bottling.
  3. finished_goods: Bottled, capped, and labeled chemical products ready for dispatch.
  4. packaging: HDPE bottles, jerry cans, 200L drums, caps, induction seals, and carton boxes.
  5. trading: Pre-packaged third-party chemical goods bought and sold directly.

3.8 Chemical Manufacturing, Formula BOM & Production Orders

Manufacturing connects raw chemicals to packaged finished goods through master recipes:

4. Non-Functional Requirements & Security

Category Requirement Specification Implementation & Verification Mechanism
Performance POS billing calculations must return in under 200ms. Database queries must execute with eager loading to prevent N+1 overhead. Benchmarked via automated test suites. Cart AJAX calculation response averaged 120ms in automated load testing.
Concurrency & Integrity Zero duplicate invoice numbers and zero race conditions on inventory decrements across simultaneous cashiers. Enforced via lockForUpdate() pessimistic database row locks on stock_balances and document_sequences.
Security & Auth Protection against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF), SQL Injection, Session Fixation, and Brute Force attacks. Laravel CSRF middleware, PDO parameterized prepared statements, Bcrypt password hashing (cost factor 12), and role-based policies.
Data Auditability Complete audit trail for stock write-offs, manual price adjustments, invoice cancellations, and user logins. Immutable stock_movements ledger and activity_logs table preserving before/after state snapshots.
